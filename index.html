WHY I'M RUNNING

Building a stronger Grand Valley for tomorrow.

I’m running for Mayor because I believe Grand Valley can protect what makes our community special while making smart decisions about the future.

This campaign isn't about criticizing the people who have served our community. It's about looking ahead and asking what we can do better — together.

We need to be thoughtful about the services we provide, how efficiently we provide them and how we grow our local economy. My goal is to build a stronger Grand Valley while keeping the residential tax burden in mind.

TAXES & VALUE

A responsible approach to taxes.

Grand Valley residents deserve to know that every municipal dollar is being used as effectively as possible. We should look at the whole picture — not simply accept year-after-year increases.

01

Review What We Offer

Look carefully at municipal services and programs. Where appropriate, reduce, change or discontinue services that aren't delivering enough value for the cost.

02

Find Efficiencies

For the services we continue to provide, look for better ways to deliver them — including smarter processes and opportunities to reduce costs.

03

Grow Our Business Base

Encourage responsible business and economic growth so new assessment and municipal revenue can help offset pressure on residential taxpayers.

04

Protect What Matters

Make decisions based on community values and long-term sustainability while maintaining the services and amenities that matter most.

GROWING GRAND VALLEY

Growth should work for our community.

A stronger commercial and business tax base can help take pressure off residential taxpayers.

That means being proactive about economic development, welcoming investment and making sure Grand Valley is positioned to compete for businesses and opportunities that fit our community.

Growth needs to be responsible and well planned. But standing still isn't a strategy either.
