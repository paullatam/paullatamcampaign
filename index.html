Why I’m Running

I’m running for Mayor because I believe Grand Valley needs strong, practical leadership.

Our community has changed, and it will continue to change. The next term should be about making responsible choices, keeping a close eye on how taxpayer dollars are spent, and planning carefully for the next stage of Grand Valley’s growth.

I want to bring a practical, fiscally responsible approach to the Mayor’s chair — one that listens to residents, focuses on results and keeps Grand Valley moving forward while protecting the community values that make this a great place to call home.

PLANNING FOR MORE GROWTH

Preparing for Grand Valley’s next stage.

Grand Valley has grown and will continue to grow, but the pace of that growth has changed.

During periods of rapid growth, the Town expanded services and made decisions based on expectations for continued development. Housing starts have since slowed, meaning some decisions may have been made ahead of when the community would actually need them.

That doesn’t mean we stop planning. It means we take stock of where we are today, evaluate the services we have, and make sure future investments match the pace and needs of Grand Valley as it continues to grow.

At the same time, we should be ready to pursue responsible business growth that strengthens our local economy and broadens the municipal tax base.
