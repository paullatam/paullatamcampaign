WHY I'M RUNNING

Our Home. Your Future.

I’m running for Mayor because I believe Grand Valley needs strong, practical leadership focused on responsible finances, thoughtful growth and the future of our community.

This election is about the kind of leadership we need to make thoughtful decisions about where Grand Valley goes next. I want to help protect the small-town character people value while making sure we are financially responsible and ready for future opportunities.

I grew up in Hillsburgh, so I know what small-town life feels like — knowing your neighbours, saying hello on the street and recognizing people when you walk downtown. I believe Grand Valley can grow and prosper without losing that sense of community.

BUDGET & TAXES

My #1 priority.

I am not going to promise that property taxes will go down. My goal is to work with Council and staff to keep increases as reasonable as possible — ideally closer to inflation where we can. That takes teamwork, difficult conversations and a careful look at how the Town spends your money.

01

Review Services

Evaluate what services we provide, whether we still need them at their current level and whether some can be reduced or changed without hurting the community.

02

Find Efficiencies

Look at how services are delivered and identify cost-saving measures, smarter processes and better ways to get the same or better result.

03

Drive Business Growth

Prepare now to attract investment to our employment lands so a broader business tax base can help reduce pressure on residential taxpayers.

PLANNING FOR GROWTH

Be ready before the opportunity arrives.

Grand Valley's employment lands can be an important part of keeping future residential tax pressure reasonable.

The pumping station is moving forward, but we should not wait until servicing is complete to start planning. We can work now on the planning framework, how those lands will be marketed and how we invite businesses to invest in Grand Valley.

Business growth should strengthen our tax base while respecting the small-town character that makes Grand Valley a place people are proud to call home.
