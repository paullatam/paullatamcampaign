WHY I'M RUNNING

A future we can all be proud of.

Grand Valley is our home. I’m running for Mayor because I believe our community deserves leadership that listens, communicates openly and is prepared to look ahead.

After five years on Council, I understand the responsibilities that come with municipal leadership. I want to bring that experience together with a fresh perspective, strong relationships and a practical approach to the opportunities ahead.

My goal is simple: to work for a Grand Valley where families, residents and local businesses can feel confident about the future.
